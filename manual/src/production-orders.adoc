= Production Orders

Factory gauges set to the xref:factory-gauge.adoc#production-orders["follow demand and order" mode] allow their products to be manually ordered from a stock keeper tuned to the same network. In the stock keeper UI, each gauge in this mode appears as a [#term-production-task]_production task_, signified by a blueprint background.

.A production task of oak planks
image::production-task.png[,310,154]

Click to add the production task to the order. The amount of output items to send can be adjusted per usual. Sending an order containing production tasks creates a [#term-production-order]_production order_, causing the corresponding factory gauges to add the number of items in the order to their xref:factory-gauge.adoc#term-demand[demands]. When the ordered items arrive in the logistic network, they are sent out again to the address specified on the order.

.Ordering 16 oak planks to be sent to the address "`output`"
image::production-order-create.png[,448,160]

The responsible factory gauge will produce enough items to satisfy the amount specified in the production order, reusing existing items in storage. For example, if the xref:factory-gauge.adoc#term-recipe[recipe] of the gauge produces 4 oak planks from 1 oak log, and 5 oak planks are ordered when 0 oak planks are in storage, then 8 planks will be produced, 5 sent to the destination address, 3 left over in storage; when 5 planks are order again, 4 new planks will be produced, leaving 2 left-over.

[#view]
== Pending Production Orders

Pending production orders can be viewed in the "production orders" tab (❶) in the stock keeper UI. They are automatically removed when all ordered items arrive in the logistic network. Otherwise, they remain stuck if the items never arrive.

.Production orders UI
image::production-orders-ui.png[,474,186]

❶ Production orders tab::
Click to switch to this tab.

❷ Destination address::
The address the items are sent to after they arrive in storage.

❸ Elapsed time::
Time elapsed since the order was created.

❹ Cancel button::
Click to cancel the order.

❺ Ordered items::
Type, amount, and status of the ordered items.

.Statuses of ordered items

[#notification]
== Delivery Notification

When all items in a production order arrive, and are dispatched to the destination address, a notification is shown on the top-right corner of the screen, to the player that has created the production order.

."Order shipped" notification
image::production-order-notify-shipped.png[,322,104]

A similar notification is shown when a responsible gauge has become <<term-invalid-gauge,invalid>>.

."Gauge became invalid" notification
image::production-order-notify-gauge-invalid.png[,322,104]

[#mod-compat]
== Mod Compatibility

Other mods may provide UIs alternative to the stock keeper for ordering items, such as handheld remote stock keepers. These UIs are likely able to create production orders without explicit support. However, they will not be able to view pending production orders, and will not display <<notification,delivery notifications>>.

The following mods are tested to work with production orders, and have explicit support to display notifications:

* Create: Mobile Packages
* Create: Phantom
